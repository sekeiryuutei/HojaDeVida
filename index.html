Thesis project · 2021

CubeLearn — Augmented Reality Educational Game

Teaching programming logic to teenagers through physical cubes and AR markers.

UnityC#Vuforia Augmented RealityAndroidGame Design

Educational mobile game designed to teach basic programming concepts through physical interaction. Players scan AR markers with their camera to trigger actions; each board challenges them to guide a fire truck by ordering markers correctly, applying sequenced instructions and loops. Built with Unity and C#, compatible with Android 4.0+.

Validation with 16 participants: 81% found the cubes fun, 62.5% rated the experience “very easy”, and 62.5% agreed that this type of game supports learning.

Want to try it? Install the APK and point your camera at the markers shown in the third image (right, left, up, down — or the loop marker to repeat a sequence).
